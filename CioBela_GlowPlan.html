✦ HOW THE GAME WORKS
Every 90 days is a new Season. Points reset each season — fresh start, new chance to climb.
Earn points by completing actions across all four categories. You must score in ALL FOUR to advance past Bronze.
Actions marked UNLIMITED can be scored every time you do them. All other actions are one-time per season.
Everyone who reaches Diamond earns their personal reward. The artist with the highest total is crowned Season Champion and gets a bonus prize on top.
You track your own progress here. Honesty is everything — this game only works if you play it with integrity.
Bonus point opportunities will be announced by the salon throughout the season. Stay tuned.
